# Product demonstration walkthrough

1. Open Overview and compare the original workbook’s 208 PASS / 32 REVIEW with the refined integrity-only 228 PASS / 12 FAIL. Inspect the disclosed 12-row trusted-reporting exclusion.
2. Filter by account or region and observe the changing numerators and denominators. Clear the filters.
3. Open Exception review and filter Rules to SUPPLIER_RECORD_MISSING. Inspect an order and compare the CRM and missing supplier source.
4. Enter a fictional reviewer, assignee and reason. Save In Review. Investigation alone leaves the critical rule active.
5. Document a root cause, corrective action and synthetic evidence. Expand the source editor, add a synthetic supplier row and confirm its status, ETA and permit values.
6. Save correction and revalidate. Then save Ready for Validation and choose Revalidate & resolve. Active rules cannot be closed.
7. Inspect the audit history, refresh the browser and find the resolved item. Export its audit JSON. A separate browser profile has its own original demonstration state.
8. Follow Source lineage and Metric dictionary to trace each number back to a source, rule, denominator and limitation.

Actual on-time performance is unavailable because verified completion dates are absent. The forecast measure must not be presented as an actual delivery outcome. Historical professional outcomes are clearly separated from these synthetic records.

Use only fictional reviewer names and synthetic evidence. Audit logs are illustrative browser-local records, not authenticated or tamper-proof business approvals.
